# ADR-0040: Place every team and project in one department, cascade the context selectors, and let a user see only what they take part in

- Status: accepted (2026-11-13, by the human in chat at mod-021 gate-3)
- Date: 2026-11-10, revised 2026-11-12 after the human's gate-1 answers
- Context: `doc-016-teams-under-departments`, stage 1 (business analysis): [spec](../features/doc-016-teams-under-departments/spec.md) BR-TD-1..-19, OQ-1..OQ-9; the human's stand feedback on mod-019, 2026-11-10, and answers at gate-1, 2026-11-12. **Supersedes in part** [ADR-0028](0028-matrix-organization-model.md) rule 1 (`ORGANIZATION ||--o{ TEAM`, `AREA_TEAM`, and project–team links across the organization), [ADR-0031](0031-typed-area-tree.md) rule 2 (a project node "or at the top level") and rule 4 (area–team links), [ADR-0032](0032-object-pages-and-navigation-context.md) § 5 (the selectors' offers and «Остальные»), [ADR-0038](0038-scoped-grants-project-managers-and-place-decides-creation.md) § 3 (top-level project creation by `organizations.area.create`); **amends** [ADR-0029](0029-scoped-effective-permissions.md) § 6 steps 3-4, § 8 item 4 and ADR-0038 § 1 (team creation). Nothing else in them changes. Only the human sets `accepted`, at the gate of the line that implements it (`mod-NNN-teams-in-departments`).

## Problem

A team is the organization's (ADR-0028 rule 1), links many-to-many to departments (ADR-0031 rule 4) and to any project of the organization, and a project may stand at the top level (ADR-0031 rule 2, ADR-0038 § 3). The human wants a strict tree, organization → department → {teams, projects}; four header selectors that cascade from the chosen parents, a lower choice filling in its parents; and a user who sees only what they take part in. ADR-0032 § 5 deliberately hides nothing and lists non-fitting values under «Остальные»; ADR-0029 shows every «Организация» project to every member, and feat-041's three visibility values were to narrow that per project. These accepted texts contradict the request, so a new ADR records the change (README, Process 3).

## Options considered

### Option 1: keep the model, filter the selectors only

- Pros: no migration, no ADR change beyond § 5.
- Cons: a team still has no single department and a project may have none, so "the teams of a department" and "choosing a team chooses its department" have no answer; sight stays organization-wide. Rejected.

### Option 2: a team as a fourth node type in the area tree

- Pros: one tree holds everything; moves, depth and names reuse the tree's rules.
- Cons: teams would enter the sibling name space beside projects, the tree lock would serialize team writes, and grants on nodes (mod-019 OQ-1 A forbids them on non-departments) would need a fifth rule. Rejected.

### Option 3: a team keeps its row and gains one required department; every project node sits under a department; links stay inside one department; selectors cascade; sight by participation (chosen)

- Pros: small model change (one required reference replaces a link relation; the top level loses project nodes); the cascade follows from the references, and a department's projects already contain its teams' projects; one sight rule for every surface, reusing ADR-0029's set form.
- Cons: an upgrade that must place teams and top-level projects by rule and cut cross-department links, items losing their team; a team link now discloses a project to the team's members; department «Менеджер» grants widen again; a linked team or project cannot move without unlinking first (OQ-9 A, open).

## Decision

Option 3. The human's answers (2026-11-12): OQ-1 A, OQ-2 B, OQ-3 A, OQ-4 A, OQ-5 A, OQ-6 A, OQ-8 "every project stands in a department" (OQ-7 moot). OQ-9 is open; § 1 states its recommendation.

**1. The tree.** An active team references exactly one department of its organization, required, keyed (organization, department) onto the area row of type `Department`; a deleted team references none (its deletion clears it), so a deleted team never blocks a department's deletion. The area–team relation is retired with its routes. A project node's parent is always a department: it is created there and moved only between departments; no project node stands at the top level, which keeps only departments. A project–team link joins a team and a project of the same department (409 `organizations.team.other_department` otherwise), decided on the locked team and project rows, so a link against a move of either end ends in one order. A team moves only while it has no project link, a project node only while no team is linked to its project (409 `organizations.team.has_links`, `organizations.area.project_has_teams`; OQ-9 A); a move then changes only the place. A department with an active team in it is not deleted (409 `organizations.area.has_teams`, checked after the department's `FOR UPDATE`). A team creation locks the tree lock and the department row `FOR SHARE`; a team move locks, in ADR-0031 rule 6's order, the tree lock and the two department rows `FOR SHARE` (tier 1), then the team row (tier 2); each ends against a department's deletion in one order, with no server error.

**2. Who creates and moves (amends ADR-0038 § 1, supersedes the top-level half of § 3).** A project is created in a department by a holder of `organizations.area.write` on it; "at the top level by `organizations.area.create`" is struck, and a request naming no department is refused 400 before any lookup. `organizations.team.create` is struck as `project.create` was: a team is created in a department by a holder of `organizations.area.write` on it. The department «Менеджер» also holds `organizations.team.write`, `.team.link` and `.team.grant` on the teams standing in its department. A team move needs `organizations.area.write` on both departments. `organizations.team.delete` stays the administrator's. The team creation route carries its place in the body and is decided as ADR-0038 § 3 decides project creation: at step 0, on the inventory's exception list, a non-admin getting 403 for any place they lack the code on (an unknown, deleted or foreign place included), the administrator «Область не найдена.» for an unknown one and 409 `organizations.area.type_not_allowed` for a project node or a sub-area. A move's destination is a body id (ADR-0029 § 7). A team code the department gives is decided on the locked team row, so a concurrent move changes the answer and never leaves a stale pass (as mod-019 BR-SA-13). Until this § lands for teams (its own line), team creation keeps `organizations.team.create`, the administrator's, with the place required.

**3. The selectors cascade (supersedes ADR-0032 § 5's offers).** The selection keeps its shape (organization, department, team, project) and the invariant: department ∈ organization; team and project ∈ department; project linked to the team when both are chosen. Offers are the visible objects under the chosen levels above; a department's project offer is the projects standing in it, which contain its teams' projects; with organization «все», every organization of the user, under a heading each. A team or a project chooses its organization and its own department; a project chooses no team. Any chosen value left unfitting resets with «Выбор сброшен: …»; a department replaced by the value's own is not a reset. «все» at a level resets every lower level. A repair (a remembered selection, a change seen while open) first gives the department the chosen team's or project's own, then resets, least specific first, each lower value that no longer fits (spec BR-TD-18); this replaces the reset direction of ADR-0032 §§ 5-6. «Остальные» retires. §§ 1, 2, 4 and 6 of ADR-0032 stand; the address still names the most specific object, so a cross-organization choice opens that object's organization.

**4. Sight by participation (amends ADR-0029 § 6 steps 3-4).** For a non-admin: a project is visible when a grant reaches it, the user is a member of a team linked to it, or its «Видим всей организации» is on; a team when the user is its member, holds a grant on it or on its department, or sees a project linked to it; a department when the user holds a grant on it or sees a team or project standing in it; an organization when the user is a member. A project node and its sub-areas are visible exactly when their project is. Step 3 gains the same gate for scopes T and A: a team or a department the user does not see answers as a missing one («Команда не найдена.», «Область не найдена.»), at § 8 item 4 and before any 403, in the path and in the `teamId`/`departmentId` query alike. Step 4 applies the organization role's bundle on every visible project, team and department, and on no hidden one. Team membership still grants no code: it adds sight, and the role then applies; a project–team link likewise gives no code but gives sight (ADR-0029's "no link widens access" reads "no link adds a code"). The project value is one boolean, off at creation; feat-041's three values are not built. No value hides a project from its linked teams' members: to restrict a project to its grantees, its node's «Наследовать доступ» is switched off, the value is off and its teams are unlinked. The set form gives, per request, the visible departments, teams and projects; every surface that names or counts them joins it before paging (ADR-0029 § 7).

**5. The upgrade, in order (spec BR-TD-19).** (1) Each active team takes its one department link; else the department holding most of its linked projects, ties by name; else the first of several links by name; else «Команды без департамента» (created or reused). (2) Each top-level project, deactivated ones too, moves into the department holding most of its linked teams as placed in (1), ties by name; else into «Проекты без департамента» (created or reused). Project names are unique in the organization, so no sibling clash arises. (3) Every project–team link left crossing departments is cut as feat-028 BR-OL-8 unlinks to «Без команды», with no actor: no «Изменён» moves, no history entry. Existing projects' «Видим всей организации» is off. Every placement, created department, dropped department–team link, cut link and item made «Без команды» is logged per organization. Additive migrations only; no merged migration is edited. Steps 2-3 may ship in a later line than step 1; they read the teams' places as they then stand.

**Departures from `docs/conventions/*`:** none.

## Consequences

- **Simpler:** a strict tree: "the teams of a department" and "the projects of a department" are one reference each, and the cascade needs no fit table across link kinds; one sight rule replaces three visibility values and «Участники областей».
- **Harder:** every multi-object read joins three visible sets instead of one; the offers' read across up to 10 organizations must stay within 2 s; a team link changes who sees a project, so linking and unlinking become access-relevant writes; moving a working team or project takes unlinking first.
- **Narrows:** doc-012's matrix: a team no longer works across departments; the upgrade cuts such links and their items lose their team.
- **Widens:** department «Менеджер» grants already given gain team codes on their department's teams (OQ-6 A).
- **Locked in:** one department per team and per project; links within a department; the cascade's invariant; participation as the default sight.
- **Revisit when:** departments nest (the cascade then has to say whether a sub-department's objects count); a team must work for two departments; an organization-wide project is needed again; sight must be granted to groups.
- **Index:** when accepted, the index lines of ADR-0028, -0029, -0031, -0032 and -0038 get "amended by ADR-0040".
